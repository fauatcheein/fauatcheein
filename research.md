## Safety and certification

A recurring thread in our work is how to *certify* autonomous agricultural machines. We develop methods aligned with ISO 18497 and ISO 25119, including simulation-based (Monte Carlo) evidence generation, so that new robots can move from research prototypes to safe deployment on farms.

## Datasets and open resources

We release datasets and code where we can.

- **Multispectral and hyperspectral leaf dataset**: chemical traits and water status of avocado, olive and grape leaves during dehydration. [Scientific Reports, 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-85714-8)
- **Generalised 2-trailer vehicle dataset**: LiDAR point clouds with last-trailer position measurements. [IEEE ICIT, 2024](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICIT58233.2024.10540788)
- **LFuji-air dataset**: annotated 3D LiDAR point clouds of Fuji apple trees under forced air flow. [Data in Brief, 2020](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2020.105248)
- **ForestYear3D**: multi-season LiDAR forest dataset collected with a mobile robot platform *(coming soon)*.
